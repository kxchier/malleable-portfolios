Title: Trays
Year: 2023
Medium: Cafeteria Lunch Trays, Found Objects
Context: The University of Chicago
Description: In Trays, 20 cafeteria lunch trays function as containers for an eclectic assortment of found objects, each meticulously arranged to resemble food. The work explores the tension between container and contents: trays associated with nourishment, childhood routine, and institutional order instead hold items that are strange, absurd, and often harmful if consumed.
