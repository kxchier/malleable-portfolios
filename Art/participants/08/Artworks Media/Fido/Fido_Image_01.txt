Title: Fido
Year: 2026
Medium: Polished Stainless Steel, Raspberry Pi 5, Diaphragm Pumps, Silicone Tubing, 3D-Printed Housing, YOLO11 Pose Detection, Loonen Bottles
Context: Designing Emerging Technologies, University of California, Berkeley
Description: Fido is a speculative, fiduciary hydration system that assigns the decision to dispense water or alcohol to artificial intelligence. Built with two reservoirs, a pump system, and a camera-linked onboard computer, Fido treats the body as input and hydration as output. To receive a drink, users must complete the One-Leg Stand field sobriety test. The project asks what it means to outsource agency to systems designed to care on our behalf.
