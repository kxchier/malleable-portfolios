Title: Painting to Dance With
Year: 2025
Medium: Canvas, steel, pine wood, various tools, Elmer's glue, India ink, oil, acrylic, graphite, colored pencil, Sharpie, dye-based ink, enamel
Context: 2025 BA Thesis Exhibition: ALL WALLS FALL, The University of Chicago. Also selected for Festival of the Arts 2025 Winter Gallery, vestige, a student-selected and student-run exhibition at the University of Chicago.
Description: Painting to Dance With emerged through an iterative conversation with materials, form, and digital tools. The work began as an abstraction of intuition, layered through experiments in scale, speed, symbolism, and rhythm. Up close, the surface reveals dense traces of human mark-making: footprints, hidden messages, small references, and accumulated gestures. From a distance, those details reorganize into a larger field of movement, inviting the viewer into a dance of approach and retreat.
