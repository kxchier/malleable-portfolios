I was always a fan of the liminal spaces / pool rooms aesthetic, but after the recent release of The Backrooms, I was inspired to create this. From my earlier experiments with Japanese bath houses, I became a fan of using water in my 3D renders. The reflections are very nice. 
Here, I add some subtle details that bring an eerie, unsettling feeling, like the floating cube, darkening hallway, and shortening windows.
